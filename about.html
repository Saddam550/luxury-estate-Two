Who We Are

Two Decades of

Founded in New York in 2004, Aurelia Estates began with a simple belief: a home of distinction deserves representation of equal distinction. Today our advisors serve clients across 40 cities, from Manhattan penthouses to Mediterranean villas.

We are a boutique by design — fewer listings, deeper relationships, and an unwavering commitment to confidentiality.
